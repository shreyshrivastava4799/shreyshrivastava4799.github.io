## About Shrey

![image](/assets/SFNYE1412.JPG){:  height="256px" width="192px" style="float:left; padding-right:20px"}
<div style="text-align: justify; font-size: 17px;"> I am a junior undergraduate from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. I have been working on the problem statement of Autonomous Ground Vehicles under the guidance of Prof. Debasis Chakravarthy where I worked on Computer Vision and Motion Planning Algorithms. I further worked under the guidance of Prof. Partha Pratim Chakraborty on Heuristic Search Algorithms. Lately I have been working on the task of Multimodal Deep Learning under Prof. Humphery Shi. I have always loved imagining curves in vector spaces and wish to work on interpretable aspects of Deep Learning.
