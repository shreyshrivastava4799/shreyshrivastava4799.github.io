## Research Interests:

My broad objective is to develop algorithms combining the perceptual power of Deep Learning with Classical AI’s reasoning capability for applications in Natural Language.

I am fascinated by the idea of conversational systems capable of acquiring knowledge and
performing reasoning in an automated way. Recent progress of deep learning in sequence
modeling has led to achieving the first part successfully. However, reasoning is a complex
task, and using neural networks for it has led to underperforming and uninterpretable
models. To tackle this, I have been working on Neuro-Symbolic AI for creating systems
capable of reasoning over even longer horizons.
